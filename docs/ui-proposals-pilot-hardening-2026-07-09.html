Mock pilot · UI approval gate

Trust should be visible
before it is beautiful.

Four focused proposals for the simulated pilot. The direction is warm, operational and explicit: a treasurer in a noisy réunion should always know what is safe on the phone, what reached the ledger, and what still needs human attention.

Mockup only — nothing below is implemented
01 · Meeting Mode

Durability expressed in human language

The existing counter only says that items are pending. The proposal distinguishes local safety from remote completion without asking the treasurer to understand “offline queues.”

  • Saved on this phone: safe to switch to WhatsApp or lock the screen.
  • Synchronizing: visible progress, no blocking spinner.
  • Needs attention: names the affected person and offers recovery.
  • Recorded: calm confirmation, then disappears.
Impact

Removes the most dangerous ambiguity in the flagship workflow: whether a green-looking tap is actually durable.

02 · Correction

Correct the ledger without erasing the past

The action appears only to the president. “Delete” is never used. The confirmation sheet explains the effective consequence before requesting a reason.

  • The original confirmation remains visible.
  • The entry becomes visibly cancelled.
  • The contribution becomes due again.
  • The president's reason is immutable and group-visible.
Impact

Makes Meeting Mode recoverable after auto-confirmation while protecting the ledger from silent edits.

03 · Passation

Move the office, not the history

The handover screen separates future responsibility from past claims. The cash figure is always attributed to a person and explicitly denies app custody.

  • Pending obligations move to Jeanne.
  • Claims already made to Marie stay with Marie.
  • Confirmed records never change parties.
  • The physical handover remains outside the app.
Impact

Prevents the new treasurer from inheriting claims they cannot truthfully confirm.

04 · Dev only

A pilot console that behaves like a control room

This surface never ships. It gives the simulated pilot one explicit place for persona switching, scenario progress and lifecycle compression—without floating controls covering production CTAs.

  • Three controllable identities: president, treasurer, member.
  • Four scripted rounds with expected outcomes.
  • Advance uses the production cron paths.
  • Reset is separated and visually destructive.
Impact

Makes every pilot run repeatable and turns simulator evidence into something another developer can reproduce.
