Recommended synthesis

Living Gathering owns the hierarchy. Borrow Collective Table’s human phrasing and handover moment. Borrow Living Ledger’s activity, confirmation, and dispute patterns.

Soft motion

100 ms press response, 180–240 ms state transitions, one focal animation at a time, native route motion, and full Reduce Motion support. No confetti or perpetual movement.

Native, not duplicated

Shared React Native product content; iOS and Android keep native navigation, sheets, pickers, menus, and dialogs. Heroicons remain the sole app-owned icon family.

Design artifact only. No production UI was changed.Light + dark · English + French safe · no custody or balance framing
