Iteration 3 — « Money hero » (dark-first)

The app answers one question the moment it opens: where does my money stand across all my njangis? A Toss/Ubank-style dark home leads with a net-position hero (owed this week vs. receiving), a quick-action row, then groups as compact dark cards with hue accents reduced to dots and ring strokes. Serious-money tone; group gradients become jewelry, not wallpaper.

Why this direction

  • Cross-group aggregation is the gap today: home currently lists groups, but "how much do I owe this week, total?" requires mental math. The hero + In/Out cells answer it instantly (data already fetched by listMyGroups).
  • Dark-first suits the market: OLED battery savings and outdoor readability matter on the mid-range Android devices most members carry; light theme remains available.
  • Hue discipline: stored colorSeed shrinks to a dot + ring stroke per group — dark surfaces make even small hue accents read strongly, keeping the one-bold-use rule effortlessly.
  • Cost: mostly theming + one aggregate section; dark palette must be tokenized once, then screens inherit. Medium effort, no schema change.

Sources: Toss dark stacked money cards · Ubank spend hero + bill planner + In & Out week · Base balance hero + quick-action row · Revolut Business total-assets widget
