Njangi · Mobbin-informed application systems

One hierarchy, three visual languages.

Each direction applies the approved action-first Today concept across Today, Actions, and Group. None uses a wallet balance or implies Njangi holds money.

Recommended

A. Priority stack

A calm utility system where the next executable step always wins. Compact rows scale the same language from Today into the full queue and group detail.

Best fit for Njangi simplicity
Improves
Scan speed, first-viewport usefulness, and continuity between Today and Actions.
Tradeoff
More operational than social; group warmth comes from identity and copy, not large imagery.
Mobbin pattern
Asana's single due-task card + Todoist's compact rows + YNAB's priority-before-summary order.
